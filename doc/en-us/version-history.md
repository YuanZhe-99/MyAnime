- `v1.5.3`: Long names you can finally read, lists that use a wide screen, and the split rule promoted out of the detail page. **The "when to split" rule was already correct; what was wrong was where it lived.** 1.5.2 derived it from the Galaxy Z Fold 8 — width ≥ 600, height ≥ 480, aspect ≥ 0.82, with the aspect test load-bearing because the Fold 8 unfolds to a 4:3 *landscape* panel and so must split in landscape and stay single-column in portrait — but it lived inside `detail_layout.dart` under detail-page names and was documented as a detail-page rule. It is now `canSplitLayout` in `shared/utils/adaptive_layout.dart`, with `useDetailTwoPane` as a one-line delegate the tests assert still agrees, and it has its own cross-cutting page at `doc/en-us/adaptive-layout.md` and a row in the AGENTS.md reading table. Once a window may split, the three data-browsing lists fill it: as many columns of at least 320 dp as fit, capped at four, laid out left to right then top to bottom. The column count follows Google's feed guidance — an adaptive minimum item width rather than a hardcoded count per breakpoint — while the gate keeps the aspect test, which is a **deliberate divergence** from Google's "use width, not aspect ratio" advice and is recorded as one: width alone cannot give the Fold 8 two different answers at one width. Each of the three modules remembers its own count in `storage_config.json`, defaulting to auto and clamped rather than rewritten when the window shrinks, so a count set on a desktop survives a fold and comes back on unfolding; the manifest already declared `screenLayout|screenSize|smallestScreenSize|density`, so that transition needs no lifecycle work at all. Management's swipe-to-edit and swipe-to-delete survive unchanged at one column and are dropped above it, because a horizontal drag inside one narrow cell is ambiguous — the new long-press sheet carries both actions anyway. That sheet is the other half of the release: every row in all three tabs truncates the title to one line, so long-pressing (or right-clicking, since holding a mouse button is not a gesture anyone makes on Windows) now shows every stored title in full, selectable and free to wrap, above **Edit** and **Delete**, with delete reusing the shared `confirmDelete` and inheriting its five-minute "don't ask again" window. `kana_page.dart`'s own hardcoded 720 is **deliberately left alone** and documented as a known exception, because routing it through the aspect rule would change its tablet-portrait behaviour and nobody asked for that. Tests went from 186 to 216. Versions unified to 1.5.3+56 / MSIX 1.5.3.0 / installer 1.5.3.
